COMMON MISTAKES:
─────────────────────────────────
❌ {{ name }}    - Has spaces
❌ {name}        - Single braces
❌ [[name]]      - Wrong brackets
❌ <name>        - Wrong brackets
❌ $name$        - Wrong delimiters
✅ {{name}}      - CORRECT

RATING CHECKBOX FORMAT:
─────────────────────────────────
Good format:  [{{q1_sd}}] SD  [{{q1_d}}] D  [{{q1_n}}] N  [{{q1_a}}] A  [{{q1_sa}}] SA

Will output:  [ ] SD  [ ] D  [ ] N  [X] A  [ ] SA

Alternative:  {{q1_sd}} SD  {{q1_d}} D  {{q1_n}} N  {{q1_a}} A  {{q1_sa}} SA

Will output:  SD  D  N  X A  SA

Choose the format that looks best in your layout.
